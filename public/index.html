The Problem & Mission

Bridging India's Critical "Care Gap"

Transforming healthcare from passive metric logging into an active life-saving infrastructure.

At CareVity AI, we are bridging India's critical "Care Gap" by transforming healthcare from passive tracking into an active, life-saving infrastructure. Designed as an all-in-one personal health caretaker, CareVity AI combines intuitive mobile application tracking, native-language voice AI assistance, and a visual 3D Digital Twin to deliver 24/7 proactive health protection, personalized coaching, and recovery management.

Capabilities

Intelligent Infrastructure Features

Built for high accuracy, seamless elder accessibility, and proactive family healthcare.

Native-Language Voice AI

Conversational assistance in Hindi, Odia, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and English, allowing non-technical family members to communicate effortlessly.

Visual 3D Digital Twin

An interactive 3D physiological model that maps symptoms, tracks organ trends, and reflects clinical changes dynamically.

Guardian Distress Escalation

Automated notifications instantly sent to designated family guardians whenever biometric patterns indicate acute shifts.

Automated Meal Insights

AI-driven nutritional analysis designed to support chronic disease management, glycemic stability, and healthy lifestyle routines.

Medication Adherence Engine

Structured dosage reminder schedules and adherence tracking to ensure recovery routines remain fully compliant with doctor guidelines.

Enterprise-Grade Security

End-to-end encrypted biometric telemetry and zero-knowledge health record protection ensuring strict family privacy standards.

Clinical Workflow

How It Works: Our 3-Step Process

A seamless loop of continuous logging, AI modeling, and proactive family protection.

STEP 01

Connect & Log

Record vitals, sleep, and meal entries seamlessly via mobile input or by speaking naturally in your regional language.

STEP 02

Continuous AI Analysis

CareVity AI builds your 3D Digital Twin, detects subtle trend anomalies, and calibrates personalized preventative routines.

STEP 03

Protect & Recover

Receive structured medication roadmaps, daily health coaching, and instant emergency alerts dispatched to family guardians.
