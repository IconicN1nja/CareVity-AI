CareVity AI Privacy Policy

CareVity AI Private Limited ("CareVity AI", "Company", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality of your personal and health-related data. This Privacy Policy governs your access to and use of our digital platforms, mobile applications, connected health devices, and digital wellness services accessible via carevityai.com.

This policy is formulated in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

01

Information We Collect

Categories of personal, physiological, and technical data collected to deliver CareVity services.

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, postal address, age, gender, and designated emergency contacts.
  • Health & Wellness Data (Sensitive Personal Data): Physiological metrics, activity levels, sleep records, vital statistics recorded through connected devices, and diagnostic records voluntarily uploaded by the user.
  • Audio & Voice Interactions: Voice inputs and transcripts generated when utilizing our conversational voice interface for navigational or informational requests.
  • Location Telemetry: Device-level geographic coordinates utilized solely for location-sensitive assistance and coordination during health events.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Device identifiers, operating system version, browser types, log files, and general performance analytics.
02

How We Use Your Information

Data processing purposes strictly aligned with user-authorized health caretaker capabilities.

  • Powering health analytics, preventive wellness insights, and chronic care tracking features.
  • Enabling interactive conversational voice features and responsive user assistance.
  • Triggering designated emergency notifications and coordinating user-requested safety protocols.
  • Maintaining platform security, authenticating accounts, and preventing unauthorized access.
03

Data Security & Storage

Enterprise-grade encryption protocols and domestic data localization.

  • Encryption: All personal and health data is encrypted during transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256 standard encryption).
  • Data Localization: In accordance with Indian regulatory standards, user data and health records are stored in secure cloud facilities located within India.
  • Access Restrictions: Access to sensitive records is restricted exclusively to authorized personnel and verified support systems on a strict need-to-know basis.
04

Data Sharing & Third-Party Access

Zero monetization of personal data — strict operational confidentiality.

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party infrastructure and logistics partners operating under strict confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations.
  • Emergency Contacts: User-designated emergency guardians or responders when emergency notifications are activated.
  • Legal Requirements: When mandated by valid legal processes, court orders, or applicable regulatory bodies.
05

Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

Comprehensive user control over data access, rectification, and erasure.

  • Access & Correction: You may review, verify, or update your personal records at any time.
  • Data Deletion & Consent Withdrawal: You have the right to request deletion of your account and withdraw consent for ongoing data processing.
  • Nomination: You may nominate an individual to manage your digital health account in the event of incapacity.
06

Contact Information

Direct communication channels for privacy inquiries and grievance redressal.
